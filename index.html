Who this is for

When there's nobody left
to hand it to.

Some weight can't go to the people around you. Not because they don't care. Because what you say to them lands somewhere, and you know it.

The call stops with you

Layoffs, a runway you can't talk about, whether to sell. Your team can't carry your doubt without it changing how they work, and you know it the second you open your mouth.

You don't want to hand it to your spouse

They'd listen. That's the problem. Every worry you set down becomes theirs to carry at two in the morning, and they've already got their own.

You're in the middle of a divorce

Everybody has a position. Friends pick sides, family has opinions, lawyers bill by the tenth of an hour. Here you can say the ugly version out loud and it goes nowhere.

Everything is changing at once

New city, new title, new diagnosis, empty house. Big change doesn't arrive as one clean feeling. It arrives as six at the same time, and they need somewhere to go.

Tomorrow is the big one

The pitch, the keynote, the interview, the toast, the conversation you've rehearsed forty times. Run it at me first. I'll listen to the whole thing without checking my phone.

Hype or vent. Your call.

Some hours you want somebody in your corner telling you you've got this. Some hours you just need to swear about it for sixty minutes. Say which one at the start and that's what you get.

Confidentiality

What you say here
stays here.

Confidential  ·  Nothing kept  ·  Not therapy

Nothing from a session gets repeated, written down, posted, or used as an example. No recordings. No notes I keep afterward. No names, ever, not even stripped of detail.

There's no paperwork to sign and no account to make, because there's nothing on my end for either one to protect. I don't keep a file on you. It's a shorter route to the same place.

If you'd rather I never learn your last name, that works too. Book under a first name and pay through Stripe. I don't need to know who you are to listen to you.

What that means, concretely

  • Nothing is recorded. Not the call, not the room.
  • I keep no notes once the hour ends
  • Your name never appears anywhere, not even as an anonymous example
  • Book under a first name if you'd rather. I don't need your last one.
  • Payment runs through Stripe, so I never see your card details
Straight with you about the one limit: I'm not a licensed therapist and this isn't legal privilege, so none of it covers a credible threat of serious harm to you or someone else. If you're in crisis, call or text 988 in the US. That's the only line I'd break, and I'd tell you before I did.
