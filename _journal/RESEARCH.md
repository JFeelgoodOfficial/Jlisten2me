# Journal research notes

advice columns, and news coverage of forum posts. The articles use the recurring details
loosely, as texture. None of them retells one person's story, and none quotes or links these
sources.

How the research was done: web search on October 13, 2026. Reddit and most other sites could
not be opened from the research environment, so everything below was seen in search-result
snippets only. Open a page before relying on it for anything public.
